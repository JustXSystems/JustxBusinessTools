Alternate direction · Soft System

Calm, soft, and effortless.

A separate exploration from the dark glass "instrument panel" system — built around the same macOS-inspired principles the brief asked for: soft shadows instead of glass or emboss, low-saturation accents, generous rounding, and gentle 150–250ms motion everywhere. Toggle Light/Dark top-right — dark mode here stays true to this system's own calm philosophy (elevation through subtle surface layering, not glow), rather than borrowing the other system's glass treatment.

00 — Brand

Same mark, new light

The Justx Systems glyph on white — it needs to hold up without the dark canvas and glow it was designed against.

10 — Login & Onboarding

Splash → sign in → home, one flow

Single-column throughout, no split brand panel. Click through it: app launch plays a logo scale-in, then "Welcome to Justx," then a Login button — that leads to the tenant's own sign-in screen, which lands on a home dashboard with the signed-in user's profile front and center by default.

This is what a user sees the moment they open the application.

14 — Dialogs & Modals

Interrupt sparingly, confirm clearly

A dimmed, blurred backdrop; the dialog scales in rather than just appearing. Destructive actions always get a coral icon and an explicit, specific question — never just "Are you sure?"

⚠
Delete purchase order PO-2291?
This will permanently remove the order and its line items. Vendors who were notified will receive a cancellation email. This can't be undone.
15 — Toast Notifications

Confirm, don't interrupt

Slides in from the top right, sits for a few seconds, slides back out. Never blocks the interface — for anything that needs a decision, use a dialog instead.

16 — Command Palette

⌘K, and you're anywhere

Every module, record, and action is one keystroke away. Grouped by intent — quick actions first, then recent records, then navigation — so the most likely match is always near the top.
